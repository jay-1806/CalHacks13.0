Quicky.AI, explained in two minutes

Quicky.AI is a desktop AI math tutor. You ask about a problem on your screen, and it explains it out loud while drawing on top of your screen.

What happens when you ask a question

The whole journey in four steps.

  1. You askHold the hotkey and speak, or type. Quicky checks that the window is not sensitive, then takes a screenshot.
  2. Quicky thinksA small local server checks privacy, remembers who you are, and looks for a similar answer it already has.
  3. Claude teachesIf there is no cached answer, Claude looks at the screenshot and writes the lesson one step at a time.
  4. You see and hear itEach step is spoken and drawn on your screen as soon as it arrives. Ask a follow-up any time.

The three code folders

Desktop app

apps/desktop
What you see: the floating launcher, the tutor panel, the screen capture and the drawing layer. Built with Electron and React.

Orchestrator

services/orchestrator
The brain that runs each turn: privacy, memory, cache, and the call to Claude. A Fastify server on port 4317.

Shared library

packages/shared
Code both sides use: data shapes, the drawing-tag parser, coordinate math, and redaction of private text.
